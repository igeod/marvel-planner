Popup

Generic modal dialogs: message, confirmation and destructive confirmation, plus the bottom sheet used on mobile. A popup interrupts the page, so it asks for one decision and nothing else. Confirmation, error, account and notification popups are catalogued below; movie and user detail popups extend this base.

Anatomy

Title in one line

Body text, one to three sentences. It explains the choice, it does not repeat the title.

Secondary Primary
  1. Scrim covers the page, ink var(--ink) at 60%.
  2. Surface var(--surface), radius 32px, padding 32px.
  3. Close 44x44 round button, top right, 12px inset.

  5. Title 24px / 800, one line when possible.

  7. Actions pills 48px, right aligned, primary last.

Copy and behavior rules

  • Title: a question for confirmations (“Reset your progress?”), a statement for errors and messages (“Could not save your list”). Sentence case, no full stop, no “Oops” or “Error”.
  • Body: what happens, then what is safe. Irreversible popups say “This cannot be undone.” Errors never show codes or stack text; put a code in small muted text under the body if support needs it.
  • Buttons: verbs that name the result. Never OK, Yes, No. Exceptions: “Got it” on information only, “Cancel” on reversible confirmations.
  • Tone by action: reversible = red or ink primary; irreversible = filled safe action plus quiet red action; error = Close plus Try again; info = single ink pill.
  • Dismissal: Esc and scrim close message and confirmation popups. Destructive, error and unsaved popups ignore scrim clicks; Esc acts as the safe action.
  • Focus: safe action in destructive and unsaved popups, primary elsewhere. Errors move focus to the popup and announce with role="alertdialog".
  • One at a time. Never stack popups. An error that appears over another popup replaces its content. A popup never opens on page load, except Session expired.
  • Loading state: after the primary is pressed it shows a spinner, keeps its width, and both actions are disabled until the result. On failure the popup turns into the Error popup.
  • Never use a popup for success of a reversible action; use a toast (see Toast and empty state).

Specification

  • Widths: small 480px (message, confirmation; ConfirmDialog), medium 560px, large 720px (reserved for detail popups). Always max calc(100vw - 32px). Height grows with content, max 100dvh - 64px, body scrolls, header and actions stay fixed.
  • Surface: light var(--surface), dark var(--raised). Radius --r-xl (sheet mobile: --r-lg solo angoli alti), padding header --pad-popup e corpo 32 ai lati (mobile 24), nessun bordo, ombra --shadow-pop.
  • Scrim: --scrim in entrambi i temi. Click on scrim closes, except destructive popups and popups with unsaved input.
  • Icon tile: 40px circle, icon 20. Message: var(--bg) neutral. Confirmation: gold var(--highlight). Destructive: red var(--accent). Lucide icons, stroke 2.2.
  • Text: title 24px / 800 / 1.2 (20px small), body 16px (15px small) in muted. Title is the aria-labelledby target, body the aria-describedby.
  • Actions: pills, 48px (44px small), gap 8px, right aligned, primary last. Max two. Labels are verbs naming the result (Mark all, Reset), never OK or Yes.
  • Close button: 44x44, top right, shown on message and large popups; omitted on confirmations, where Cancel does the job.
  • Behavior: real dialog with showModal(), role="alertdialog" for destructive. Focus moves in on open (to the safe action in destructive, else the primary), is trapped, and returns to the trigger on close. Esc closes. Page scroll locked, page behind inert.
  • Motion: scrim fade --dur-enter, surface from scale .96 / 8px down to rest in --dur-enter, ease-out var(--ease-out); exit --dur-ui. Sheet slides up from the bottom. None under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Mobile: under 640px becomes a bottom sheet; actions stack full width, primary on top; sheet closes on swipe down and scrim tap.
  • Components: Popup (props: size, tone: message | confirm | destructive, title, children, actions, onClose). Detail popups compose the same shell.
