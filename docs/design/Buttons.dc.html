Rules. Hover styles only inside @media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine). Focus ring is :focus-visible, appears instantly (no transition), 3px, offset 2 to 3px; ring color is the opposite of the surface (gold on dark and red surfaces except on gold, where it is ink; red on light). Keyboard-triggered actions are never animated beyond the focus ring. Reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce): remove transform animations (press scale, chevron rotation, knob slide, sliding pill, check drawing), keep a 0 to 80ms color or opacity change, keep the spinner but slow it to a pulse of opacity. Disabled buttons: opacity .45, cursor: not-allowed, no hover, no active; use disabled or aria-disabled. Loading: aria-busy="true", keep the label and width (no layout shift), ignore clicks.

aria-disabled="true"Background --disabled, text --on-disabled, cursor not-allowed. No hover, active or transform. Stays focusable with the standard focus ring; a click shows the reason in a role=status.
